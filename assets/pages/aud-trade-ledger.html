Dividend income is kept separate from capital gains — it's assessable income, not a capital gain, and each payment is converted at its own payment-date rate.
Exchange rates are a fixed reference table baked into this page (daily AUD/USD, Jul 2025 – Jul 2026); AUD = USD amount ÷ rate for that date, using the most recent available rate on or before the trade date.

Realized P/L is not the broker's USD P/L simply re-priced at one rate. Each closing trade is FIFO-matched against the specific lot(s) it opened — a purchase for a long position, or the original short sale for a short position. Each leg is converted at its own transaction date's rate, then netted in AUD. This correctly handles both directions: for a long position, "Basis Matched" is the cost paid (a debit); for a short position it's the credit received when the position was opened (shown as a negative figure, not a cost) and is subtracted the same way in the formula. This captures FX movement between the two dates — a trade flat or negative in USD can still show an AUD gain or loss, and vice versa, purely from the exchange rate moving between them.
